# AIDLC
- AI driven development life cycle or AI Native Development Life Cycle.
- AIDLC is operating model for building software where AI agents 
    - plan
    - code
    - test and 
    - manage systems
- Human developers shift into roles focused on governance, intent-setting and validation.

# Three Phases of AIDLC
- Inception 
- Construction
- Operations

## Inception
- AI Analyzes business intent, user meetings and goals.
- Transforms raw concepts into structured requirements, user stories and execution plans.
- Humans actively validate, question, and refine context before any coding begins.

## Construction
- AI Agents propose system architecture, domain models, and write massive blocks of code.
- Developers act as editors and architects, clarifying technical choices in real time.
- Traditional multi week sprints shrink into fast bolts measured in hours or days.

## Operations
- AI Handles infrastructure as code, deployment tracking and predictive monitoring.
- AI flags root causes and regressions proactively instead of teams reacting to late night alerts.
